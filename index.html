Niche or Die

A word game where the obvious answer gets you killed. Everyone gets the same category. The rarer your answer, the taller your tower, and the lava is rising.

  1. A category appears, like "Name a fruit". The timer starts.
  2. Type an answer. Common answers earn a block or two. Rare ones earn a stack.
  3. The lava climbs every round. The last player standing wins.

The studio

Small team, clear ideas

Chez Games is an independent studio building for Roblox. We start from games players already love, find the one rule worth changing, and build the whole experience around that change.

Everything is made in Roblox Studio and shaped by feedback from the people who play it.

  • Easy to startA new player should understand the game before the first round ends.
  • Fair to playSkill and cleverness decide who wins, not who spent the most.
  • Built with playersUpdates follow what the community asks for.
